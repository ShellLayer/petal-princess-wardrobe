# 美术素材与生成记录

本页记录 1.0–2.0 的素材。3.0 新增中国人物与 30 套中国风衣装等内容，当前美术记录和完整新提示词见 [3.0 美术与提示词](v3/美术与提示词.md)。

本项目使用内置图像生成工具制作图集：初版角色和背景两幅，2.0 新增基础裙装、独立发型两幅。没有调用外部收费 API、购买素材或引用现有动画角色。生成于 2026 年 11 月 14 日。

| 文件 | 内容与尺寸 | 运行方式 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| assets/princesses-original.png | 1086 × 1448，RGBA 透明 PNG，3 列 × 2 行，共六套裙装 | 保留原始输出 |
| assets/scenes-original.png | 1536 × 1024，三联场景 | 保留原始输出 |
| assets/princesses.webp | 初版带固定发型、鞋子的角色图集 | 保留历史素材，新版不再加载 |
| assets/princesses-base-v2-original.png | 从原角色编辑生成的六套基础裙装，1086 × 1448 透明 PNG | 保留原始输出 |
| assets/princesses-v2.webp | 新版基础裙装图集 | 与发型、鞋子独立组合 |
| assets/hairstyles-v2-original.png | 六种透明发型，1055 × 1491 | 保留原始输出 |
| assets/hairstyles-v2.webp | 六种发型，透明补边为 1056 × 1492 后编码 | 按统一位置叠加 |

新基础裙装由内置生图工具编辑原角色，移去固定发型和鞋款。生成后原始输出保留在项目中，素材脚本只做切片、透明补边和 WebP 编码。发型按统一头部坐标摆放，后发使用同一张发型图的局部纹理填充；鞋子、头饰、面罩、翅膀与手持物分别叠加。画面没有运行时 AI、骨骼动画或物理模拟。

2.0 两次调用的最终完整提示词见 [生图提示词-v2.txt](生图提示词-v2.txt)。两次均使用内置生图工具与 `transparent_background: true`，未使用 CLI / API 回退路径。编辑参考均为本项目自己生成的原图。

## 角色图集：最终完整提示词

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: production sprite sheet for a beautiful gentle princess dress-up web game for a three-year-old child.
Create ONE transparent PNG sprite atlas, 3 columns by 2 rows, six equally sized rectangular cells. Portrait-format overall canvas if possible, 1536x2048. Each cell contains the SAME original, adorable fairytale princess girl, full body, facing forward, with identical face, skin, long flowing warm chestnut brown hair, soft rounded facial features, big kind brown eyes, small smile, childlike storybook proportions (head roughly one fourth full height), arms relaxed slightly apart, hands visible at hips. Modest age-appropriate princess dresses with sleeves. NO crown, NO headband, NO hair ornaments, NO hand-held objects. All six girls must be identical in pose, face, hair silhouette and size; ONLY dresses and matching slippers change. Perfectly centered inside each of the six cells, 10% empty margin all around. Hair top at 8% and shoe bottom at 93% of each cell. No elements cross cell boundaries.
Top left outfit: blush pink rose princess ball gown, softly scalloped skirts, delicate tiny embroidered roses and pearl trim.
Top center: powder blue cloud and tiny silver-star princess gown, translucent-looking layered tulle.
Top right: lavender lilac moonflower gown, flowing soft pleats, tiny golden moon embroidery.
Bottom left: warm ivory and butter yellow sunbeam gown, daisy lace and delicate gold trim.
Bottom center: pale sage green woodland gown, leaf embroidery and soft ivory lace.
Bottom right: peach coral butterfly gown, delicate translucent layered butterfly-like skirt with small floral motifs, NO wings.
Style: premium contemporary children's fairytale picture book illustration, delicate gouache and colored pencil finish, subtle luminous watercolor shading, clean graceful silhouette, lovely textile detail, gentle high quality studio character art, cohesive warm pastel palette. Beautiful and charming rather than plastic 3D, not flat clip-art. Crisp cutout edges and REAL transparent alpha background including between the limbs. No background, ground, shadow, frame, grid lines, checkerboard pattern, lettering, labels, numbers, watermark or logos. Original character, no existing franchise.
```

该提示词使用 `transparent_background: true`，实际返回尺寸为 1086 × 1448，已核实存在透明通道；没有为了匹配期望尺寸拉伸图集。

## 背景图集：最终完整提示词

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: background triptych sprite atlas for an elegant children's princess dress-up browser game.
A single wide landscape illustration containing THREE exactly equally wide vertical panels arranged side by side, each panel a self-contained portrait scene with NO borders, gaps, text, lettering, watermark or characters. All panels use the same contemporary premium children's picture-book style: soft luminous gouache and watercolor, lovely botanical details, very subtle paper grain, warm pastel colors, atmospheric light. The CENTRAL 55 percent of EACH panel is almost empty, airy, light and low contrast, intended behind a large full-body doll. Dense decorative details ONLY around outer edges, top and lower ground of each panel. Flat level gently lit floor at bottom 18 percent for standing.
LEFT panel: enchanted rose garden, elegant green leafy arch framing the upper edges, abundant soft blush pink and ivory garden roses at sides and bottom, ivory distant garden, warm sunny soft cream center, pale stone path in lower middle, tiny distant butterflies. Romantic botanical garden, sage and muted green.
MIDDLE panel: airy fairytale palace terrace, tall pale ivory and peach columns at edges, open delicate arched doorway, gauzy pale rose curtains, distant golden cream palace towers through opening, sunlit cream marble floor with tiny pale pink petals, soft powder pink and butter yellow, empty center.
RIGHT panel: magical twilight moon garden, periwinkle and lavender plants and hydrangeas at bottom and sides, slender elegant tree branches along upper edges, small pale golden crescent moon in upper corner, tiny softly glowing fireflies, luminous pale lavender blue center and low stone path, dreamy but not dark or frightening.
Original artwork, delicate and lush, painterly, premium editorial finish. Not 3D, not flat vector. Each panel has matching horizon and camera height. Overall 3:2 landscape canvas suitable for three portrait backgrounds. No dolls, no people, no furniture in center.
```

该提示词使用 `transparent_background: false`。背景根据舞台容器比例自适应铺满，角色保持 1:2 图集单元比例。
